# Signal Lanterns

## Genre

Cinematic indie electronica / festival-lift electronic with organic percussion and nostalgic vocal-chop energy.

## Mood

Warm, yearning, airborne, triumphant, and night-drive emotional without turning gloomy.

## BPM

105

## Key / Scale

D major with suspended color and a bright, uplifted center.

## Structure

- intro: 8
- groove: 16
- lift: 8
- breakdown: 16
- return: 16
- outro: 8

## Prompt References

- Song or artist: ODESZA - "A Moment Apart"
  Borrow: cinematic depth, vocal-chop shimmer, patient uplift, wide emotional return
  Avoid copying: exact melody, exact sound design, exact pacing of the breakdown
- Song or artist: ODESZA - "Late Night"
  Borrow: forward percussion motion, nostalgic synth glow, human-feeling vocal punctuation
  Avoid copying: exact bass movement, exact hook contour, exact mix balance

## Sonic Goals

- start with a soft cinematic bed before a clear rhythmic entrance
- use clipped percussion and vocal-chop punctuation to create lift, not just extra hats
- keep the drop/return emotional and wide rather than aggressive
- make the hook feel like a memory fragment instead of a full lead vocal
- use warmth and motion instead of brittle top-end brightness

## Review Goals

- make sure the return really blooms after the breakdown
- keep the groove moving without becoming a generic house loop
- preserve warmth in the low mids while keeping the kick and bass readable
- avoid over-busy melodic writing or EDM riser clichés
