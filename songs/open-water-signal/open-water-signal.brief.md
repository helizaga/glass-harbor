# Open Water Signal

## Genre

Emotional progressive house / melodic electronica with a slow-blooming, late-night arc.

## Mood

Weightless, yearning, hypnotic, patient, and euphoric by the end.

## BPM

120

## Key / Scale

A minor center with open suspended color and long melodic release.

## Structure

- 16-cycle intro
- 16-cycle pulse
- 8-cycle lift
- 16-cycle breakdown
- 16-cycle bloom
- 16-cycle afterglow
- 8-cycle outro

## Sonic Goals

- very gradual emotional build rather than an early drop
- soft but present four-on-the-floor foundation once the groove arrives
- long pads, repeating hook fragments, and spacious delay tails
- restrained vocals and textures that feel human without taking over
- cinematic lift into a wide, emotional bloom section

## Review Goals

- keep the long-form build compelling instead of static
- make sure the bloom section lands clearly after the breakdown
- avoid brittle hats or overly aggressive top-end lift
- preserve warmth and space even when the full groove opens up

## Stable Sound Roles

- `kick_main`
- `clap_main`
- `hat_closed`
- `hat_open`
- `perc_top`
- `impact_wide`
- `riser_up`
- `shimmer_fx`
- `air_texture`
- `vocal_chop`
