# Filter Memory Carousel

## Genre

Discovery-era French house study with disco/funk pulse and loop-first arrangement logic.

## Mood

Glossy, buoyant, robotic, nocturnal, and playful without becoming cartoonish.

## BPM

122

## Key / Scale

D minor center with bright borrowed color in the loop movement.

## Structure

- 8-cycle intro
- 16-cycle groove
- 8-cycle lift
- 8-cycle breakdown
- 16-cycle drop
- 8-cycle outro

## Sonic Goals

- make the song feel built from one strong loop idea rather than many harmonic sections
- emphasize filter motion and mute choreography over cinematic risers
- keep the kick and bass pocket dry, steady, and danceable
- use robotic accents and clipped synth punctuation instead of emotional vocal hooks
- make the return section feel stronger because of subtraction, not because of sheer density

## Review Goals

- keep groove continuity strong enough that the track still feels like a DJ-friendly strut
- make the filter-open section clearly audible as timbral evolution, not just louder hats
- make the breakdown feel subtractive and intentional instead of empty
- keep the return section punchy and pocketed rather than festival-sized
- watch for muddy kick and bass overlap

## Stable Sound Roles

- `kick_main`
- `clap_main`
- `hat_closed`
- `hat_open`
- `perc_top`
- `impact_wide`
- `riser_up`
- `shimmer_fx`
- `air_texture`
- `vocal_chop`

## Notes For Agent

- this is a one-off study experiment, not a permanent repo style package
- keep the style reference in the brief only, not the song metadata
- use public scaffold sounds and browser synths only
- favor loop-first repetition, filter movement, and robotic punctuation
- avoid melodic-house wash, festival risers, cinematic sprawl, and overcomplicated harmony
