# Neon Heartline

## Genre

EDM crossover / melodic dance-pop with a clean pocket, immediate hook, and a bright but controlled return.

## Mood

Hopeful, charged, replayable, romantic, and night-drive vivid without turning syrupy.

## BPM

124

## Key / Scale

A minor center with a little C-major lift in the return.

## Structure

- intro: 8
- groove: 16
- lift: 8
- breakdown: 8
- return: 16
- outro: 8

## Example Targets

- none

## Prompt References

## Sonic Goals

- make the hook feel immediate in the first 30 seconds
- let the return feel big through width and weight, not clutter
- keep the low end clean and steady under the kick
- use vocal chops as emotional punctuation instead of a literal topline
- avoid loud default browser synth character

## Review Goals

- protect hook memorability without bright harshness
- keep kick and bass separated when the return opens up
- make the breakdown feel like a real valley before the payoff
- keep the arrangement compact enough to replay cleanly
