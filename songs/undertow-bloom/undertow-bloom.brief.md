# Undertow Bloom

## Genre

Emotional progressive house / melodic electronica with a long, patient bloom.

## Mood

Wide, aching, nocturnal, suspended, and cathartic without turning festival-bright.

## BPM

121

## Key / Scale

D minor center with suspended and open voicings.

## Structure

- 16-cycle intro
- 16-cycle pulse
- 8-cycle lift
- 16-cycle breakdown
- 16-cycle bloom
- 16-cycle release
- 8-cycle outro

## Sonic Goals

- long-form emotional build with a very sparse breakdown
- stronger first-hit bloom than the earlier attempts
- warm kick and bass weight without making the low end muddy
- restrained brightness, with lift coming from width and motion instead of harsh tops
- clear impact and riser resolution into the bloom

## Stable Sound Roles

- kick_main
- clap_main
- hat_closed
- hat_open
- perc_top
- impact_wide
- riser_up
- shimmer_fx
- air_texture
- vocal_chop

## Review Goals

- improve breakdown-to-bloom contrast materially over the earlier attempts
- keep the emotional shape patient and cinematic
- make the bloom feel earned rather than merely louder
