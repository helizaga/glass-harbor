# Chrome Night Service

## Genre

Discovery-era French house study with disco-loop discipline, robotic punctuation, and filter-driven arrangement.

## Mood

Glossy, playful, nocturnal, and confident with a machine-funk pulse rather than emotional sprawl.

## BPM

123

## Key / Scale

D minor center with bright modal color in the chord stabs.

## Structure

- intro: 8
- groove: 16
- filter-open: 8
- breakdown: 8
- return: 16
- outro: 8

## Prompt References

- Song or artist: Daft Punk - "One More Time"
  Borrow: loop-led momentum, filter-open lift, buoyant four-on-the-floor energy
  Avoid copying: exact vocal hook, exact chord cadence, exact drop timing
- Song or artist: Daft Punk - "Voyager"
  Borrow: bass-and-drum lock, clipped disco-funk motion, playful robotic confidence
  Avoid copying: exact bassline, exact lead contour, exact arrangement details

## Sonic Goals

- make one chord-stab loop carry most of the arrangement instead of many sections with new harmony
- keep the kick and bass pocket dry and steady enough to feel DJ-friendly
- use filter movement, mute choreography, and robotic punctuation as the main source of lift
- make percussion feel tight and danceable rather than busy
- keep the return stronger because the breakdown subtracts aggressively, not because of giant FX

## Review Goals

- preserve groove continuity across the whole track
- make the filter-open section audibly brighter and wider without turning harsh
- make the breakdown feel intentionally subtractive, not empty
- keep the return punchy and pocketed instead of festival-sized
- watch for bass and kick overlap or too much hat-driven generic house feel
