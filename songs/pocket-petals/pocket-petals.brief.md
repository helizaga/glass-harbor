# Pocket Petals

## Genre

Airy UK garage / bedroom dance-pop study with hook-forward vocal fragments and soft club energy.

## Mood

Weightless, wistful, fluttery, intimate, and bright without becoming sugary.

## BPM

129

## Key / Scale

B minor center with rosy major-color turns in the hook.

## Structure

- intro: 8
- glide: 16
- hook: 8
- breakdown: 8
- return: 16
- outro: 8

## Prompt References

- Song or artist: PinkPantheress - "Passion"
  Borrow: featherlight vocal-fragment hook energy, fast but soft groove, wistful sweetness, compact arrangement
  Avoid copying: exact melody, exact vocal phrasing, exact bass movement, exact chord cadence

## Sonic Goals

- make the song feel compact and instantly memorable instead of cinematic
- use vocal-chop fragments as the emotional hook rather than a big synth lead
- keep the drums light and skippy, with a garage-pop bounce instead of a heavy house stomp
- make the bass warm and supportive rather than dominating the track
- keep the return sweet and replayable rather than oversized

## Review Goals

- preserve a quick, featherlight groove without losing punch
- keep the hook front-and-center even with minimal harmonic material
- avoid muddy kick and bass overlap in a fast arrangement
- make the breakdown short and useful, not a full reset
- avoid drifting into generic house or festival-lift structure
