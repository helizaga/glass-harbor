## Sonic Goals

- make the groove feel soft and expensive rather than bright and busy
- keep the hook memorable with small ingredients instead of a huge lead
- let the low end feel warm and steady without turning cloudy
- use vocal chops as emotional punctuation, not as a full lead
- keep the arrangement compact, human, and replayable

## Review Goals

- protect low-end separation while keeping warmth
- keep the groove settled and lightly syncopated instead of stiff
- make the hook feel immediate without over-layering
- avoid drifting into full house, glossy EDM, or sleepy no-drums ambient drift
