# Velvet Turnsignal

## Genre

Garage-pop / late-night club crossover

## Mood

Playful, flirtatious, punchy, and more direct than the recent deep-house drafts

## BPM

130

## Key / Scale

A minor

## Structure

- intro: 8
- groove: 16
- lift: 8
- breakdown: 8
- return: 16
- final-return: 16
- outro: 8

## Hook Identity

- primary hook carrier: chopped `vocal_chop` phrase with a short `pluck_pitched` answer
- supporting answer: clipped `stab_pitched` chord stabs that fill the offbeats without washing the mix

## Low-End Plan

- what owns the sub / bass pocket: `bass_pitched`
- how should kick and bass trade space: bass answers the kick and avoids sitting under every downbeat
- should the bass prefer `bass_pitched` or `bass_tonal`: `bass_pitched`

## What To Withhold Until Return

- save this element for the return: the densest vocal-hook pattern and the widest open-hat motion

## Breakdown Subtraction Plan

- remove: kick weight, most hats, and the main bass answers
- keep: one vocal fragment, soft chord punctuation, and a thin air bed
- first thing to re-enter: kick + one clear vocal-hook downbeat

## Musical Inferences

- make the reset feel obvious by leaving the atmospheric deep-house lane entirely
- prefer a broken-grid club feel over a patient bloom
- keep the arrangement shorter, punchier, and more hook-forward

## Capability Gaps

- the runtime pack is stronger at drums, chops, and tonal fragments than at premium topline vocals
- this should aim for memorable and playable, not for a fully polished commercial topline record

## Sonic Goals

- primary sonic goal: one sticky vocal-led hook over a swung, broken two-step pocket
- use `vocal_chop` as both airy bed and hook punctuation
- let the main answer come from `pluck_pitched`
- prefer chopped sample identity, displaced groove, and orbit-separated space over pad bloom
- let groove variation come from `struct()`, `swingBy()`, `degradeBy()`, and light phrase transforms

## Review Goals

- keep the hook obvious without crowding the low end
- make the return hit harder than the intro/groove without piling on too many new parts
- avoid brittle top end and obvious browser-synth identity
