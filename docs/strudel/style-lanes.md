# Style Lanes

Use this to keep different songs from collapsing into the same house-adjacent accent.

## Atmospheric Deep House

Prefer:

- `bass_pitched`, `stab_pitched`, `pluck_pitched`, `air_texture`
- `voicing()`, `rootNotes()`, `seg()`, `orbit()`
- dominant roles: `pluck_pitched`, `bass_pitched`
- support roles: `stab_pitched`, `air_texture`

Avoid:

- overly busy vocals
- brittle hats
- all layers blooming at once
- prune first: `impact_wide`, `riser_up`, `hat_open`

## UK Garage Pop

Prefer:

- `vocal_chop`, `bass_pitched`, `pluck_pitched`, `hat_closed`
- `swingBy()`, `late()`, `chooseCycles()`, `slice()`, `lastOf()`
- dominant roles: `vocal_chop`, `bass_pitched`
- support roles: `pluck_pitched`, `hat_closed`

Avoid:

- deep-house wash
- constant sub sustain
- too many simultaneous hooks
- prune first: `air_texture`, `shimmer_fx`, `impact_wide`

## French House

Prefer:

- `stab_pitched`, `bass_pitched`, `vocal_chop`
- `clip()`, `end()`, `struct()`, filter motion, `chunk()`
- dominant roles: `stab_pitched`, `bass_pitched`
- support roles: `vocal_chop`, `clap_main`

Avoid:

- melodic-house pad beds
- too much harmonic drift
- giant EDM riser dependency
- prune first: `air_texture`, `hat_open`, `shimmer_fx`

## Moombahton / Latin Club

Prefer:

- `vocal_chop`, `bass_pitched`, `perc_top`, `impact_wide`
- `struct()`, `degradeBy()`, `chooseCycles()`, `slice()`, `orbit()`
- dominant roles: `vocal_chop`, `perc_top`
- support roles: `bass_pitched`, `impact_wide`

Avoid:

- airy pad-first writing
- too much harmony
- soft deep-house pocket
- prune first: `air_texture`, `shimmer_fx`, `hat_open`

## Downtempo Pop / Soft Club

Prefer:

- `vocal_chop`, `pluck_pitched`, `bass_pitched`, `air_texture`
- `slice()`, `clip()`, `early()`, `chooseCycles()`, `seg()`
- dominant roles: `vocal_chop`, `pluck_pitched`
- support roles: `bass_pitched`, `air_texture`

Avoid:

- loud default synth leads
- huge trailer impacts
- overbuilt breakdowns
- prune first: `impact_wide`, `riser_up`, `hat_open`
