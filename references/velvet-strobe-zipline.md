## Active Sound Roles By Section

- intro: `kick_main`, `air_texture`
- groove: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, bass lock
- lift: `hat_open`, clipped hook fragments
- breakdown: bass ghosts, filtered bed, robotic answer
- drop: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, `hat_open`, `impact_wide`

## Groove Notes

- kick and bass should feel quantized together before anything else matters
- transitions can snap through cuts and micro-pauses instead of long sweeps
- robotic phrases should answer the groove, not take over it

## Harmony Summary

- keep the center stable and let timbre, cutoff, and phrasing provide personality

## Transition Recipes

- micro-pause before the return
- filtered bass ghost during the breakdown

## Reusable Takeaways

- pocket is the style anchor
- if the bass and kick lock, the track can stay harmonically simple
