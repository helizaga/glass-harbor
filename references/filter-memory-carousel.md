## Groove Notes

- the loop should feel already alive before the section count gets bigger
- movement comes from filter opening and mute choreography more than from new chords
- robotic punctuation should feel clipped and playful, not sentimental

## Harmony Summary

- one strong harmonic loop with small color shifts is better than multiple unrelated sections

## Transition Recipes

- filtered groove squeeze before the return
- one-bar dropout or subtraction before the impact downbeat

## Reusable Takeaways

- let repetition feel intentional through timbral change
- a strong return can come from restoring withheld information, not from adding everything
