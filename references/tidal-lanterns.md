- pulse: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, tonal bass
- lift: `hat_open`, `riser_up`
- breakdown: pads, sparse hook fragments, `air_texture`
- bloom: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, `hat_open`, `perc_top`, bass, `impact_wide`
- release: groove core plus reduced tops

## Groove Notes

- bass stays patient until the bloom
- hats widen gradually instead of all at once
- clap anchors the emotional lift
