## Active Sound Roles By Section

- intro: drum spine with narrow filtered loop
- groove: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, `perc_top`
- lift: `hat_open`, brighter loop fragments
- breakdown: filtered fragments, robotic response, no full clap
- drop: restored full groove plus one brighter withheld layer

## Groove Notes

- keep the drum pulse steady while the loop phrasing changes around it
- subtraction should feel like a breath with memory, not a hard genre switch
- the brightest layer should be withheld until the return

## Harmony Summary

- lock to one central vamp and let rhythm edits create most of the contrast

## Transition Recipes

- narrow-band filter before the break
- clap and bass return together on the first bar of the comeback

## Reusable Takeaways

- make contrast through absence and restoration
- let one bright return justify the whole build
