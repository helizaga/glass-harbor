## Energy Curve

- low -> medium -> medium -> medium-low -> low -> medium-high -> low

## Active Sound Roles By Section

- intro: `air_texture`
- groove: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, tonal bass
- breath: `shimmer_fx`, sparse tops
- breakdown: pads, light vocal punctuation, `riser_up`
- drop: same groove core plus `hat_open`, `perc_top`, `impact_wide`

## Groove Notes

- groove should feel smooth before it feels heavy
- onset density rises gradually instead of all at once
- open hats feel like lift, not aggression
- claps stay dry enough to anchor the groove
- percussion moves around the clap, not against it

## Harmony Summary

- stable minor center
- slower harmonic rhythm than melodic progressive material

## Transition Recipes

- shimmer swell into a quieter breakdown
- impact used sparingly for the biggest section only

## Reusable Takeaways

- keep low end warm, not oversized
- use texture and spacing to create sophistication
- save the widest FX moments for the final drop
- let bass support atmosphere before it starts driving aggression
