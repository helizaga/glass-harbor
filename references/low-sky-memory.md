## Active Sound Roles By Section

- intro: `air_texture`
- groove: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, tonal bass
- groove-2: groove core plus subtle upper motif
- breakdown: pads, sparse hook return
- drop: groove core plus `hat_open`, `perc_top`

## Groove Notes

- reuse one motif instead of adding many new parts
- changes should be small but cumulative
- bass and hats do more structural work than lead overload

## Harmony Summary

- narrow harmonic movement with repeated voicing memory

## Transition Recipes

- motif echo into breakdown
- quiet re-entry before full drop groove

## Reusable Takeaways

- long songs can evolve through repetition with variation
- hold back novelty until it matters
