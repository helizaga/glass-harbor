# Reference: Melodic Progressive House Blueprint

## Tags

- melodic house
- progressive house
- cinematic
- restrained
- polished

## BPM Range

- 122-126

## Mood

- reflective
- warm
- patient
- euphoric

## Section Map

- intro: 8
- groove: 16
- lift: 8
- breakdown: 16
- drop: 16
- outro: 8

## Energy Curve

- low -> medium -> medium-high -> low -> high -> low

## Active Sound Roles By Section

- intro: `air_texture`, `shimmer_fx`
- groove: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, tonal bass
- lift: `hat_open`, `perc_top`, `riser_up`
- breakdown: pads, plucks, `air_texture`, vocal punctuation
- drop: `kick_main`, `clap_main`, `hat_closed`, `hat_open`, `perc_top`, bass, lead, `impact_wide`

## Groove Notes

- hats stay controlled in the groove and widen in the drop
- clap is stable; tops and open hats drive motion
- kick remains simple while bass density changes energy

## Harmony Summary

- minor tonic center
- 4-chord loop with suspended color
- brighter voicing in the breakdown

## Transition Recipes

- 8-bar riser into 1-bar impact
- hat pullback before the drop
- filtered air bed under the breakdown

## Reusable Takeaways

- let density, not complexity, drive drop contrast
- keep tops polished instead of aggressively bright
- use atmospheric beds to keep breakdowns cinematic
