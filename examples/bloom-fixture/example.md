# Bloom Fixture

## BPM / Range

118-122

## Tags

- bloom
- emotional
- long-build
- patient

## Why You Like It

- the payoff feels delayed and earned
- the return opens emotionally without turning into clutter

## Target Qualities

- clear valley before the bloom
- strong return payoff
- spacious lead and pad interplay
- controlled low end

## Anti Goals

- flat section contrast
- brittle high end
- overstuffed drop

## Section Notes

- anchor: atmosphere with implied motion
- groove: pulse should stay patient
- breath: remove enough weight to make the bloom arrive clearly
- return: widen and brighten without losing low-end control

## Groove / Low-End / Transition Observations

- low end should stay controlled when the bloom opens
- the return should feel bigger through contrast and width, not extra layers everywhere
- keep top-end lift smooth instead of sharp
