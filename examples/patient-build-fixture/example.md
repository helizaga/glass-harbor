# Patient Build Fixture

## BPM / Range

116-122

## Tags

- progressive
- patient
- night-drive
- spacious

## Why You Like It

- the track keeps momentum without rushing the payoff
- the arrangement stays readable even when it gets bigger

## Target Qualities

- patient groove
- readable arrangement
- gradual brightness lift
- sustained emotional tone

## Anti Goals

- abrupt payoff
- too much harmonic drift
- weak groove continuity

## Section Notes

- anchor: establish tone quickly without overcommitting the rhythm
- groove: make the motion feel stable and repeatable
- breath: carve space before the biggest section
- return: re-enter with confidence, not surprise clutter

## Groove / Low-End / Transition Observations

- groove should feel stable enough to survive repetition
- transitions should be gradual until the final return
- low end should remain supportive rather than dominant
