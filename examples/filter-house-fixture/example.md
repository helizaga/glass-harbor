# Filter House Fixture

## BPM / Range

118-124

## Tags

- loop-house
- filtered
- robotic
- restrained

## Why You Like It

- the groove feels locked before the return arrives
- the movement comes from filter staging and subtraction more than harmonic sprawl

## Target Qualities

- tight drum and bass pocket
- loop-first momentum
- filter motion over chord churn
- clear subtractive breakdown

## Anti Goals

- cinematic wash
- oversized riser stack
- busy melodic top line

## Section Notes

- anchor: sparse setup with texture and implied pulse
- groove: drums and bass settle early
- breath: subtraction makes room for the return
- return: the payoff wins by re-entry clarity, not clutter

## Groove / Low-End / Transition Observations

- kick and bass should feel glued, not smeared
- syncopation should support the pocket instead of fighting it
- transitions should feel earned by removal and filter opening
